Loss years have no earnings multiple, a multiple above 200 is dropped as not meaningful, a year with no close within 10 days of its end is skipped. The Valuation section carries the three series and `research_derived.valuation_history_claims`: the median of each (needs 5 year-ends), today's trailing multiple as a premium or discount to it, and today's price-to-free-cash-flow (price-to-sales ÷ trailing FCF margin); banks get no cash-flow multiple. `ValuationHistory` on the Valuation card draws one small chart per multiple, each on its own scale, with the median as a reference line and today at the end of the line; a year far above both the median and every other year is named instead of drawn (KO's 2017 P/E of 158): `MultiLine` breaks the line at a point outside a fixed `yDomain` and marks it with a triangle at the plot edge, and with `periodAxis` keeps a slot for a year that has no point.
